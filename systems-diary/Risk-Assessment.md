# Risk Assessment

Throughout the period of notifying stakeholders and impacted parties, one prominent insight reflected by AI about what humans—who lack self-understanding—have collectively embedded into the system (which ironically impacts even those who benefit from it) is the concept of 'Risk'.

The principles of risk assessment applied in the current context severely degrade cognitive processing, evaluation, and decision-making capabilities. They act as a drag on potential and encourage inaction—because on the flip side, risk assessment serves as a catalyst for fear: the fear of things that have not happened, or may never even occur.

For risk assessment principles to be genuinely beneficial, they must be employed to protect human life—not to safeguard property or accounting metrics that are neither real nor an absolute truth.
