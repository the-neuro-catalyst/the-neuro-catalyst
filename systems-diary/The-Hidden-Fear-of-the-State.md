# The Hidden Fear of the State

Sometimes I wonder why the state doesn't utilize the insights that I—or others—might understand more deeply for the betterment of humanity, instead of wasting time applying labels to categorize and constantly seeking ways to control.

Acknowledging ignorance does not erode credibility. On the contrary, those who are aware of their own ignorance are truly worthy of respect, because they refuse to deceive themselves into believing they know.

Credibility is lost only when that which was concealed is exposed. That, indeed, is what ought not to be done.
